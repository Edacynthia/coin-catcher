# Coin Catcher

A small three.js game for start.ng. Catch coins, dodge bombs, grab gems.
You play as your own start.ng character, and your scores go on the game's leaderboard.

- `index.html` is the whole game (it loads three.js from the jsDelivr CDN)
- `startng.json` describes the leaderboard columns (Score, Best streak)
- `cover.png` is the picture on the game's card on start.ng
